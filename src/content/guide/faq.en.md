# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about managing tracks, email notifications, and account settings.

---

### ❓ When are automated email reminders sent?
Our scheduler automatically scans all active tracks every evening at **8:00 PM (20:00)**. If you have any track that hasn't been checked-in for over 24 hours, a single summary email will be sent to help you protect your daily Streak.

---

### ❓ What happens if I miss a check-in day?
If you miss a day, your Global Streak resets to 0. However, **your total EXP points, Level, and unlocked Badges are never lost**! You can resume check-ins anytime to build a new streak.

---

### ❓ What is the difference between Public and Private tracks?
- **Public Tracks**: Accessible via link so friends or colleagues can view your progress.
- **Private Tracks**: Strictly confidential and visible only to you when logged in.

---

### ❓ How do I share my streak on Facebook or Twitter?
Navigate to your **Profile** page (`/profile`). Click the **Share FB** or **Share X** button next to your display name to generate a shareable post with your streak stats!

# Gamification: EXP, Levels & Streaks

Multitrack incorporates game design mechanics (Gamification) to turn daily learning into an addictive, rewarding habit.

---

## 🔥 Global Streak Rules

Your **Global Streak** measures how many consecutive days you have performed at least one check-in across any of your active tracks.

- **Active Streak**: Performing a check-in within **24 hours** extends your streak by +1 day.
- **Overdue Warning**: If a track hasn't been checked-in for over 24 hours, its status changes to **Check-in Needed** and an automated reminder email is dispatched at 8 PM.
- **Streak Protection**: Keep your streak alive to unlock milestone Badges!

---

## 🛡️ Profile Levels & EXP Formula

Every action you take in Multitrack grants **Experience Points (EXP)**:

| Action | EXP Reward |
| :--- | :--- |
| Daily Track Check-in | **+150 EXP** |
| Resolving a Knowledge Gap | **+100 EXP** |
| Completing a Milestone | **+200 EXP** |
| Earning a Badge | **+300 EXP** |

### Level Threshold Formula
Your profile level progresses dynamically according to the quadratic formula:
$$\text{Min EXP for Level } N = N \times (N - 1) \times 50$$

- **Level 1**: 0 EXP
- **Level 2**: 100 EXP
- **Level 3**: 300 EXP
- **Level 5**: 1,000 EXP
- **Level 10**: 4,500 EXP

## 🏆 Achievements & Badges

As you maintain your momentum, you unlock prestigious achievement Badges displayed on your **Profile**:
- 🥉 **First Check-in**: Log your very first study note.
- 🔥 **Streak Master (7 Days)**: Maintain a 7-day global streak.
- ⚡ **Level 5 Novice**: Reach Level 5.
- 👑 **Feynman Scholar**: Resolve 10 knowledge gaps.

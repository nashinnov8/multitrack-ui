# Getting Started with Multitrack

Welcome to **Multitrack** — your personal learning tracker designed to help you master multiple skills in parallel without overwhelm.

---

## 🚀 Quickstart Guide (3 Simple Steps)

### Step 1: Create a Learning Track
A **Track** represents a major goal or skill journey you are currently building (e.g., *IELTS 8.0 Intensive*, *React System Design*, or *Morning Fitness Routine*).

1. Navigate to your **Dashboard**.
2. Click the **+ Create New Track** button at the top right.
3. Enter a title, a short description, and select whether you want to make it **Public** or **Private**.

---

### Step 2: Define Milestones & Concepts
Break down your track into manageable chunks:
- **Milestones**: Major checkpoints (e.g., *Complete Chapter 1-5*, *Score 7.5 on Mock Speaking*).
- **Concepts**: Key knowledge items you need to explain using the **Feynman Technique**.

---

### Step 3: Perform Daily Feynman Check-ins
Consistency is key to building lasting habits:
1. Click the **Check In** button on any active track card.
2. Select the concept you studied today.
3. **Explain it simply** as if teaching a 12-year-old child.
4. If you stumble or use complex jargon, flag it as a **Knowledge Gap (Gap)** to review later.

> 💡 **Tip**: Every check-in rewards you with **+150 to +250 EXP** points, increases your profile level, and extends your **Global Streak 🔥**!
